# QA 2026-11-10: phone and tablet audit (Studio "Ray a pet")

How it was tested:
- **Runtime (Studio Device Emulator):** iPhone 13 (844×390, usable 749×388 after the safe insets) and
  iPad 10th gen (1180×820), touch input, the real game in Play. A layout script read every HUD piece,
  Roblox's thumbstick and jump button, every visible text and every button (`AbsolutePosition`,
  `AbsoluteSize`, `TextBounds`), and popups were opened by tapping their tiles.
- **Code:** every client controller that draws UI or reads input, plus the server's remote limiter.
- **Not done:** no physical phone or tablet. Multi-touch, lost touches, real network lag and frame rate
  on a cheap Android need a real device (owner item, listed at the end).

Labels: **[C]** confirmed in the emulator, **[I]** found by reading the code, **[P]** possible, needs a real device.

## Bug reports

### M-01: popup close X is too small to tap
- **Severity:** High · **Device:** Phone
- **Location:** every popup's red X, `PopupController.open` (`closeB`)
- **Description:** the X is 27×20 px on an iPhone 13 (Apple's minimum is 44×44).
- **Reproduction:** emulator iPhone 13, tap the world, open Pets, measure `Popups.Popup.Panel.Face.Close`.
- **Expected:** a thumb-sized close target. **Actual:** 27×20 px; misses hit the header instead.
- **Root cause:** the X is `headerHeight × 0.8` of a popup that is itself only ~231 px tall on a phone (M-02).
- **Affected files:** `src/client/Controllers/PopupController.luau` (`PopupController.open`).
- **Fix:** an invisible hit area at least `Config.ui.minTouchPx` square around the X, plus the bigger phone popup (M-02).
- **Regression risk:** low; the hit area sits on the header ribbon, which has no buttons.

### M-02: popups are too small on phones
- **Severity:** High · **Device:** Phone
- **Location:** every popup; `Screen.popupFrame`, `PopupController.open` (`SMALL`)
- **Description:** on iPhone 13, row text is 8.8 px ("Common", "In pen", "Stored", the Welcome back line),
  "Equip best" is 108×17 px, "In pen" 61×21 px, "Collect" 92×22 px. The 2026-11-03 QA logged this as PT-05.
- **Reproduction:** iPhone 13, open Pets / Welcome back; run the layout script.
- **Expected:** readable text (≥ 11 px) and buttons near thumb size. **Actual:** see above.
- **Root cause:** the popup is 70% of the HUD layer's height (231 px of 330) and starts below a band kept
  for the island banner, which never shows while a popup is open. Reward popups shrink a further 0.62×.
- **Affected files:** `Screen.luau` (`popupFrame`), `PopupController.luau` (`open`).
- **Fix:** on phones the popup uses the whole layer height minus the side inset (about 1.35× bigger),
  and short reward popups shrink to 0.8× instead of 0.62×. The content is all Scale, so it grows with it.
- **Regression risk:** medium: every popup's look on phones (re-checked after the fix). PC and tablet unchanged.

### M-03: a lost touch freezes the ray's input
- **Severity:** High · **Device:** Both
- **Location:** `RayController.Start` (input), `pointer`
- **Description:** the ray follows one finger (`pointer`). It's cleared only by that finger's `InputEnded`.
  If that event never comes (app sent to the background mid-hold, the Roblox menu opened with a finger down,
  a system gesture), `pointer` stays set: every later tap on a pet is ignored, and the ray keeps aiming at
  the old screen spot, shooting whatever walks under it.
- **Reproduction (device):** hold a finger on the world, swipe home / pull down the notification shade, come back, tap a pet.
- **Expected:** the next tap shoots. **Actual (by code):** nothing, until a mouse click.
- **Root cause:** no reset on focus loss / menu open; no check of the touch's own state.
- **Affected files:** `src/client/Controllers/RayController.luau`.
- **Fix:** drop the pointer when its touch reads `End`/`Cancel`, on `WindowFocusReleased` and on `GuiService.MenuOpened`.
- **Regression risk:** low; a held shot ends when the game loses focus (as letting go would).

### M-04: long banner messages are tiny on phones
- **Severity:** Medium · **Device:** Phone
- **Location:** the message banner, `Screen.layout` (`banner`)
- **Description:** the banner is 194×34 px on iPhone 13. "A Heat Critter grabbed your cube! Zap it back"
  and "… is out! Pan full: it's in Storage" wrap into two lines of about 10 px.
- **Reproduction:** iPhone 13; set the banner's text to those lines and read `TextBounds` (done by script).
- **Expected:** one readable line. **Actual:** two ~10 px lines.
- **Root cause:** width = 50% of the screen's short side, which is narrow on a wide phone.
- **Affected files:** `Screen.luau`.
- **Fix:** on phones the banner is at least `Config.ui.bannerPhoneWidth` (62%) of the layer's width.
- **Regression risk:** low; it sits under the (hidden) island banner and above nothing else.

### M-05: menu tiles are small tap targets
- **Severity:** Medium · **Device:** Phone
- **Location:** the tile grid, `Screen.layout` (`tiles`)
- **Description:** each tile's pressable face is 40×36 px on iPhone 13.
- **Reproduction:** iPhone 13, layout script, `Hud.Main.Tiles.*.Face`.
- **Expected:** ≥ 44 px. **Actual:** 40×36.
- **Root cause:** tiles are 10.5% of the short side (388 px → 41 px).
- **Affected files:** `Screen.luau`, `Config.luau` (`ui.minTouchPx`).
- **Fix:** on touch screens a tile is at least `minTouchPx` (46) px. PC unchanged.
- **Regression risk:** low; the grid is still pushed up clear of the coins (checked by script).

### M-06: popups cover the tile grid on 4:3 tablets
- **Severity:** Medium · **Device:** Tablet
- **Location:** `Screen.popupFrame`; Pets, Daily, Settings, Thaw, Pan Upgrades, Tester, reward popups
- **Description:** iPad 10th gen: the Pets popup spans x 163-1015, the tiles end at x 209: the second tile
  column (Shop, Daily, Tester) is cut by the panel.
- **Reproduction:** iPad 10th gen, open Pets.
- **Expected:** the popup sits clear of the HUD it keeps on screen. **Actual:** 46 px overlap.
- **Root cause:** 80% width × 1.6 aspect assumes a 16:9 screen; on 4:3 it reaches the tiles.
- **Affected files:** `Screen.luau`, `PopupController.luau`.
- **Fix:** popups that keep the HUD are capped to the width between the tiles and the mirrored right margin.
  Big popups (Equipment, Index, Sell, Rebirth) hide the HUD and keep their size.
- **Regression risk:** low; on tablets those popups get ~14% smaller (text still ≥ 18 px).

### M-07: popups don't refit when the screen size changes
- **Severity:** Medium · **Device:** Both (iPad split view / Stage Manager, Android resize, window resize)
- **Location:** `PopupController.open` (`px`), `Ui.corner`
- **Description:** opened on the phone size, then the emulator switched to iPad: the Pets popup grew but its
  text stayed phone-sized ("Equip best" tiny inside a big button).
- **Reproduction:** open Pets on iPhone 13, switch the emulator to iPad.
- **Expected:** the popup redraws for the new size. **Actual:** stale text sizes and corner radii.
- **Root cause:** text sizes are pixels worked out once when the popup opens.
- **Affected files:** `PopupController.luau`.
- **Fix:** when the popup layer's size changes, the open popup is rebuilt (debounced, same data, no extra "ShopOpened").
- **Regression risk:** low; a list's scroll position resets on a resize.

### M-08: a camera-drag finger blocks shooting with a second finger
- **Severity:** Medium · **Device:** Both
- **Location:** `RayController.Start` (`InputBegan`, Touch branch)
- **Description:** the first finger on the world becomes the ray's pointer even when it is only turning the
  camera; a second finger tapping a pet is then ignored.
- **Reproduction (device):** drag the camera with one finger and tap a pet with another.
- **Expected:** the tap shoots. **Actual (by code):** nothing.
- **Root cause:** `and not pointer`: only the first touch counts.
- **Affected files:** `RayController.luau`.
- **Fix:** a new touch takes over the pointer when the current one isn't locked on a target.
- **Regression risk:** low; a finger that is already shooting keeps the pointer.

### M-09: every camera drag throws Slow Gel
- **Severity:** Medium · **Device:** Both
- **Location:** `GearController.Start` (`InputBegan`)
- **Description:** while Slow Gel is held, any touch that starts on the world throws a puddle, including the
  start of a camera drag, so turning the camera wastes the throw and starts the 10 s cooldown.
- **Reproduction (device):** hold Slow Gel, drag to turn the camera.
- **Expected:** only a tap throws. **Actual (by code):** a drag throws too.
- **Root cause:** throws on `InputBegan` for `Touch`.
- **Affected files:** `GearController.luau`.
- **Fix:** on touch, throw on `UserInputService.TouchTap` (a short tap without dragging); mouse and R2 unchanged.
- **Regression risk:** low.

### M-10: a world prompt hold can stay held
- **Severity:** Medium · **Device:** Both
- **Location:** `PromptController.show` (the pill's `InputBegan` / `InputEnded`)
- **Description:** the hold ends only on the pill's own `InputEnded`. A finger that slides off the pill, or a
  touch Roblox cancels, may never end it, so a hold prompt (paying at a gate) completes after you let go.
- **Reproduction (device):** press a gate's pill, slide off it, lift.
- **Expected:** the hold stops when the finger lifts anywhere. **Actual (by code):** may keep filling.
- **Root cause:** no global end for the touch that started the hold.
- **Affected files:** `PromptController.luau`.
- **Fix:** end the hold on `UserInputService.InputEnded` for that same input too.
- **Regression risk:** low.

### M-11: the compact "Pick up" prompt is tiny on phones
- **Severity:** Medium · **Device:** Phone
- **Location:** `PromptController.show` (`compact`)
- **Description:** world prompts are 9.5% of the short side tall (37 px on iPhone 13); the compact one is
  0.7× that: 26 px.
- **Expected:** ≥ 40 px on touch. **Actual:** 26 px (computed from the code and the measured viewport).
- **Root cause:** sizes are only a share of the short side.
- **Affected files:** `PromptController.luau`.
- **Fix:** on touch screens prompts are at least `minTouchPx` tall (compact: 40 px); width keeps its ratio.
- **Regression risk:** low; PC unchanged.

### M-12: one remote budget for everything
- **Severity:** Medium · **Device:** Both (worse on mobile networks)
- **Location:** `Remotes.allowed` (server)
- **Description:** every remote shares 12 calls/s per player. Aiming alone may send ~10/s, and a phone
  whose packets bunch up after a lag spike delivers many calls at once, so real calls get dropped quietly:
  a dropped "Aim none" keeps the server freezing after you let go; a dropped "SetMenuOpen false" leaves the
  server thinking a menu is open.
- **Reproduction:** Studio network simulation with high latency, shoot while dragging (needs a 2-player / lag test).
- **Root cause:** one shared bucket.
- **Affected files:** `src/server/Services/Remotes.luau`, `Config.luau` (`player.rateLimits`).
- **Fix:** Aim gets its own bucket (20/s); everything else keeps 12/s.
- **Regression risk:** low; Aim is already checked and throttled by FreezeService.

### M-13: hotbar slot name is small
- **Severity:** Low · **Device:** Phone · **Location:** `Screen.layout` (`hotbar`)
- **Description / actual:** "Ice Ray" renders at 10.4 px in a 46 px slot. **Expected:** ≥ 11 px.
- **Root cause / fix:** slot = 12% of the short side; on touch it's at least 52 px. Tutorial card moves up with it.
- **Affected files:** `Screen.luau`. **Regression risk:** low (card / jump button clearance re-checked).

### M-14: corner radius fixed at build time
- **Severity:** Low · **Device:** Both · **Location:** `Ui.corner`
- **Description:** the shared radius is pixels from the screen size at build time; after a resize HUD
  blocks keep the old radius. **Fix:** corners register in a weak table and update on a viewport change.
- **Affected files:** `src/client/Lib/Ui.luau`. **Regression risk:** none visible.

### M-15: respawn during a show moves the borrowed camera
- **Severity:** Low · **Device:** Both · **Location:** `CameraController` (`frame`)
- **Description:** `frame()` sets the camera on spawn even while a show owns it (Scriptable): one frame jumps.
- **Fix:** skip the framing while the camera is borrowed. **Affected files:** `CameraController.luau`.
- **Regression risk:** none.

### M-16: `Config.ui.scale` is never used
- **Severity:** Low · **Device:** Both · **Location:** `Config.ui.scale`
- **Description:** UI Rules say popups use UIScale 1 / 1.2 / 1.35 per device; nothing reads it (popups are
  shares of the screen instead). **Fix:** removed; the phone popup layout (M-02) replaces it; UI Rules note added.
- **Affected files:** `Config.luau`, vault `UI Rules.md`. **Regression risk:** none (unused).

### M-17: FPS average every frame for everyone
- **Severity:** Low · **Device:** Both · **Location:** `PopupController.Start` (`RenderStepped`)
- **Description:** a per-frame FPS average runs all session; only the tester readout shows it.
- **Fix:** it runs only while the Tester panel is open. **Affected files:** `PopupController.luau`. **Risk:** none.

### M-18: Tester tile label 8.8 px
- **Severity:** Cosmetic · **Device:** Phone · Testers only. Grows with M-05; otherwise left as is.
