# QA 2026-11-17: every device (Ice Ray for Pets, Studio "Ray a pet")

**Verdict: GO.** 9 bugs found, 9 fixed, each fix re-checked in the game. Two owner decisions and the "Not covered" list below stay open.

Page with the tickets and screenshots: https://claude.ai/artifact/DUvJyVDyTy3ANwtdbnvQpD

## How it was tested

- **Studio Device Emulator**, the real game in Play, driven over Studio MCP:
  - **PC:** "Current device" (1348×536) and HD 1080 (1920×1080). Real mouse clicks on the HUD tiles, top buttons and menu buttons; a real mouse hold on a pet.
  - **Phone:** iPhone 17 Pro (874×402) and Samsung Galaxy S22 Ultra (772×360, the smallest preset, 677×338 usable).
  - **Tablet:** iPad 10th generation (1180×820).

- **Layout script** on every device: every visible text and button (size, text height, off screen, under the Roblox top bar), with all 14 menus opened through the Studio `DevOpen` hook.
- **Fresh save** (`Config.dev.freshSave = "qa1117a"`, QA store) on the phone: the whole tutorial, start to finish, through the game's own remotes and prompts at a kid's pace.
- **Exploit script:** 4,260 junk and spammed calls to every remote.
- **Code review** of input handling (touch, gamepad, keyboard) and of each fix.
- Before testing, Studio was 13 scripts ahead of the repo. Studio's copies were merged into the repo (3-way, nothing lost) and the repo's newer Config pacing pass was synced into Studio, so the test ran on the latest game.

### PT-01: gamepad Select did nothing
- **Where:** `src/client/Controllers/InputController.luau`, the "menu" action (`Config.ui.gamepadKeys.menu = ButtonSelect`).
- **Repro:** PS5 / Xbox, press Select (View) to reach the menu tiles. Nothing happens. The binding sank the press and no script listened to "menu", so Roblox's own selection toggle never ran either.
- **Fix:** Select puts the selection on the top menu tile, pressing it again lets go. Skipped while the tiles are hidden (tutorial, popup).
- **Checked:** code; with `DevDevice` gamepad every menu puts the selection on a button when it opens. Not pressed on a real controller (test scripts can't press gamepad buttons).

### PT-02: Shop on a controller started on a Robux Buy
- **Where:** `PopupController.luau`, `shop()` set `o.first` to the pulsing Buy.
- **Repro:** PS5 emulator, open the Shop: the selection is on the "2x Coins 199" Buy. A opens Roblox's purchase prompt.
- **Fix:** the Buy still pulses, but the controller lands on the X (the Thaw Machine already follows this rule: A never starts a purchase).
- **Checked:** Shop now selects `Close.Face`; Pets, Settings unchanged.

### PT-03: Equipment unreadable on phones
- **Where:** `EquipmentPopup.luau`, `statBar` and `gunCard`.
- **Repro:** Galaxy S22 Ultra, open Equipment: stat numbers 24×6 px boxes, 6.4 px text.
- **Fix:** on phones the stat rows show the name and the number only (no bar), each twice as wide, and the stat panel is taller (preview 0.33 → 0.24).
- **Checked:** stat numbers 10.4 px in 62×11 px boxes on the smallest phone; screenshot.

### PT-04: Pan Upgrades ran over its title on phones
- **Where:** `PanPopup.fill`: the list only scrolled with more than 3 cards; with Look gone (2026-11-17) there are 3, so the centred list overflowed both ways.
- **Repro:** Galaxy S22 Ultra, Pan Upgrades: Slots card over "Pan Upgrades".
- **Fix:** always a scrolling list.
- **Checked:** header 16–49 px, list from 55 px; screenshot on phone and PC.

### PT-05: standing inside the Thaw Machine
- **Where:** the machine's deck (`MapArt.thawMachine`) was open to walk on.
- **Repro:** walk at the Thaw Machine (the tutorial's arrow points there). You end up on the pad under the halo; the camera is pulled to 2.6 studs and the screen is the machine's glass.
- **Fix:** `PenService.machinePrompts` adds an invisible `PadBlock` cylinder over the deck (40 studs tall, CanQuery off so the camera and the ray ignore it). A first 8.5-stud one was jumped onto, hence the height. The machine's own top is kept in the model's `TopY` attribute so the timer sign over it doesn't move.
- **Checked:** walking in from 3 sides stops 4.4 studs from the middle; Thaw still works from there; timer sign above the machine; screenshot. No map rebuild needed.

### PT-06: second Thaw Machine walk-through for its owner
- **Where:** `PanLooks.styleSecond` set `CanCollide = false` on every part whether the machine was shown or not.
- **Repro:** reach Slots level 3 (second machine), walk into it: you pass through (your character's physics is on your own client).
- **Fix:** each part's own CanCollide is kept in `BaseCollide` and restored once the machine is yours.
- **Checked:** Slots 3: 121 parts styled, 96 solid on the client, 96 on the server.

### PT-07: banner over Tutorial Complete
- **Where:** banners live on the Alerts layer (DisplayOrder 6), over the popups (5).
- **Repro:** phone, finish the tutorial by opening the Pine Forest gate.
- **Fix:** the Alerts layer waits while the Tutorial Complete card is up.
- **Checked:** card clean; Alerts back on after OK; screenshot.

### PT-08: owned guns showed their price
- **Fix:** an owned or held gun shows only Owned / Holding (wider chip). **Checked:** screenshot.

### PT-09: leaderboard row overlap on phones
- **Where:** the board popup placed the height-sized circles at fixed shares of the width; phones' rows are taller for their width.
- **Fix:** rank, avatar and name are placed from the row's real size (same spots as before on PC).
- **Checked:** rank 165–209, avatar 214–258, name from 263 px; screenshot.

## Open: owner decisions

- **DR-01, small phones:** on the Galaxy S22 Ultra some text is still 9–10 px (the iPhone-size phones are 10.4 px or more): Rebirth's KEEP / RESETS / YOU GET lists (the columns were shortened on phones on purpose, so the Rebirth button is thumb-sized), HUD tile labels 9.6 px, gun tile names 8.8–9.6 px, Sell tile names 8.8 px, Daily / Index counters 9.6 px. Rule is 11 px. Fixing Rebirth means a layout choice: fewer items per column, or a smaller button.
- **DR-02, tutorial pet runs away:** step 1–2 says "Hold your finger on the pet", but the tutorial pet flees like any other (6 studs/s). Held from 9 studs without following it, it got away and its ice melted back to 0. A 7-year-old has to chase with a finger down. **Decided (owner, 2026-11-17): keep it running.** No change.
