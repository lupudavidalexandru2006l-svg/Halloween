# Pacing pass 2026-11-16: coin prices for "easy first hour, then a grind"

Owner, 2026-11-16: study Steal an Egg's coin prices and set our core-loop prices so the first hour is easy (retention) and it gets harder after about an hour. Sketch: progress shoots up fast, then flattens.

Simulator: `tools/pacing_sim.py` (a typical kid on the Config numbers, 8 runs each; also a fast and a slow kid). It replaces the lost `balance_sim.py` and the out-of-date `ServerStorage.GrindBot` (it still uses the removed Training Rink and has no Thaw Machine or trainer).

## 1. What Steal an Egg does (fan wikis, Nov 2026)

| Ladder | Prices (cash) | Step |
|---|---|---|
| Trails (speed x1.5 to x14) | 100, 5K, 75K, 1.5M, 30M, 750M, 20B, 500B, 12.5T, 300T | x15-25 (first step x50) |
| Treadmills (+2 to +2,000 speed a step) | free, 15K, 250K, 5M, 120M, 3B, 75B, 3T, 50T, 1Q | x17-25 |

| Pet income (Forest, Common to Legendary) | $1-2, $8, $35-45, $180-240, $1.8K a second | about x5 a rarity |

What we take from it:
1. **Two ladders, offset.** Each ladder goes up about x20 a step, but they sit between each other: 100 (trail), 5K (trail), 15K (treadmill), 75K (trail), 250K (treadmill), 1.5M (trail), 5M (treadmill)... So the next buy is only x3-5 away and there is always something to save for. Ours has six ladders (guns, trainers, Thaw Speed, gear, Looks, Slots) plus rebirths; the pass interleaves them the same way.
2. **The first buy is nearly free** ($100 trail with $1-2/s pets). Ours: Slots 2 at 50 coins, about 30 s in.
3. **Round prices only** (1, 1.2, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7.5 x a power of ten). We already do this (`Config.nice`).
4. **Gates are steady (about x4), the money ladders are steep (about x20).** Ours: island needs climb steadily; coin prices climb faster than income late, which is what makes the grind.

## 2. The problem before this pass

A typical kid bought **everything in about 4.5 h**: Rebirth 1 at 18 min, Rebirth 2 at 36 min, island 10 at 3.2 h, then nothing new after hour 4. The first hour was a good feast, but the rebirths came too early and the game ran out. The vault's Balancing Rules want Rebirth 1 at 30-40 min, Rebirth 2 at 60-75 min and at least 10 h of main progression.

## 5. Limits
- This is a model of a kid, not real play. The model's own numbers: walks x1.6 the straight line, 6 s of fuss a trip, 2.5 clicks a second at the trainer at 70% focus, picks the best of 3 pets in view, sells half the pets it doesn't keep. Night, the Big Chill, events, Index pages, friends, daily gifts and challenges aren't modelled (they all speed things up a little).
- Check it the vault's way (Balancing Rules 6): a fresh-account playtest and a tester log for the first hour, and the progression funnel in analytics after launch.

## 6. Rarity pass 2026-11-17: Steal an Egg's rarities, fitted to our 8 pets

Owner: "study the pets rarities in Steal an Egg and apply them to ours" (sizes come next), and "we only have 8 animals per island so adapt the rarities".

**What Steal an Egg does.** Ten rarities (Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic, Cosmic, Secret, Eternal, Divine), eight pets per biome egg. In one biome a pet earns about **x4-x5 the rarity below**, with a **x10 jump from Epic to Legendary** and x10-x15 steps above Mythic (Jungle: Rare $90-110, Epic $420, Legendary $4.8-5.5K, Mythic $22-28K, Secret $3.5M a second). Later biomes drop the low rarities, so each biome's eight pets sit higher up the ladder. Mutations: Silver x1.2 (6%), Golden x2.5 (4%), Rainbow x3.5 (1%). Size: income x (weight / base weight)^(37/60), Giant about x3, Huge about x5.

**What we kept.** Our eight pets an island already cover seven rarities (two Commons, then one of each), so no new rarities and no new pets. We took the **income steps**, tamed:

| Rarity | Odds (unchanged) | Income, island 1 (before) | Income, island 1 (now) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Common | 60% (two pets) | 1 | 1 |
| Uncommon | 25% | 3 | **4** |
| Rare | 10% | 10 | **20** |
| Epic | 4% | 30 | **100** |
| Legendary | 0.9% | 100 | **500** |
| Mythic | 0.1% | 400 | **2,500** |
| Secret | 1 in 20,000 | 1,500 | **25,000** |

**Why tamed, and why our odds.** Steal an Egg's odds are per egg, and a player carries one egg at a time from nests that restock every 5 minutes; ours freeze a pet about every 20 seconds. Copied exactly (their odds 50 / 30 / 12 / 5 / 2.5 / 0.5 % and the x10 Legendary step), the simulator's luckiest 10% of kids earned 10-30x the unluckiest 10% (it was 2-4x), so luck decided who hit walls. The tamed ladder (x4-x5 steps, Secret x10 Mythic as the jackpot, our odds) keeps that spread at about 3-7x early on. The owner picked it.

**Prices re-tuned** (`tools/retune_prices.py`, then smoothed by hand) so the timeline stays the one approved in sections 3-4: everything after the first 3 minutes costs more because pets earn more.

## 7. Sizes 2026-11-17: Big, Giant, Huge

Owner: "look into the sizes chances and benefits and implement that to our game: bigger size, but rare like in Steal an Egg".

**What Steal an Egg does.** Every egg rolls a weight (shown in kg), apart from its mutation, so the two stack. A heavier pet earns more: income x (weight / the pet's base weight)^(37/60), slower past 125x (2x the weight = x1.53, 10x = x4.1, 100x = x17). Its players call the x3 ones "Giant" and the x5 ones "Huge". Heavier eggs take longer to hatch. Size odds aren't published; fan guides say a size is easier to get than a rare colour mutation (Rainbow 1%), and a really heavy roll needs a few server hops.

**What we built** (`Config.sizes`, rolled by `WildPetSpawner` when a wild pet spawns, saved as `sz` on each pet, nil = normal; every save from before is all normal pets):

| Size | Chance | Coins | Drawn (pet and cube) | Thaw | Freeze |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Big | 1 in 20 | x1.5 | x1.25 | x1.25 | x1.1 |
| Giant | 1 in 100 | x3 | x1.6 | x1.6 | x1.25 |
| Huge | 1 in 1,000 | x5 | x2 | x2 | x1.5 |

- The coin bonuses follow Steal an Egg's weight curve (x1.5 is about twice the weight, x3 about 6x, x5 about 13x), and use their Giant x3 and Huge x5. Size stacks with the mutation and the pose: a Huge Golden Frostfox earns 1,000 a second on island 1 (a normal one 100).
- Rarer than Golden (1 in 40) at the top: a Huge is a story to tell, and a Huge Secret is 1 in 20 million.
- Shown everywhere: the name tag ("Huge Golden Snowbun", in the size's colour: Big teal, Giant orange, Huge red), the Pets list (a size word after the rarity), the Sell screen (sizes sell as their own stack), and the catch card (a fifth SIZE cell, and EARNS counts it).
- Carried and pan cubes grow with the pet but stop at 1.4x (`Config.carrySizeMax`), so a stack never hides you and the pan grid stays clear.
- Testers: `givePet(key, mut, size)`.

**Pacing check** (`pacing_sim.py`, sizes on): Rebirth 1 at 35 min, Rebirth 2 at 75 min, island 10 at 8.4 h, last buy at 10.6 h, so prices stay. The longer thaws eat most of the extra coins. Luck spread stays at 3-5x in the first half hour. Tried and dropped: Big 1 in 10 / Giant 1 in 50 / Huge 1 in 500 (not rare enough for the owner's ask).

**Checked in Studio (2026-11-17):** 20,000 real spawner rolls gave Big 1,031, Giant 204, Huge 25 (expected 1,000 / 200 / 20); a normal, Big, Giant and Huge Waddles stood side by side at 2.2 / 2.75 / 3.5 / 4.4 studs with their coloured tags; no script errors. Not yet seen live: the Pets list row, the Sell stack and the catch card with a sized pet.

**All pets 25% bigger (owner 2026-11-17: "the commons are too small, the differences were good before"):** `Config.petScale` 1 -> 1.25, and the ice cubes grow with it, so every pet and cube is the same 25% bigger and the steps between S / M / L and Big / Giant / Huge stay as they were (S 2.75, M 3.75, L 5 studs next to the 5.8-stud avatar). A first try that made each rarity 5% bigger than the one below was undone. Checked in Studio: the island-1 line-up beside the player, no script errors. One number to change if they should be bigger still. Then another 25% (owner: "make everything 25 percent bigger"): `Config.petScale` 1.5625, so S 3.4, M 4.7, L 6.25 studs (an L pet now stands a little taller than the avatar), Huge L 12.5. Checked in Studio beside the player, no script errors.

## 10. Late-game pass 2026-11-17: each stage harder than the last

Owner: "make sure the late game is harder than at the start, study Steal an Egg".

**What Steal an Egg does.** Costs grow geometrically while rewards grow slower: trails and treadmills cost about x15-25 a step, rebirths about x3.5 each (a fan calculator: 250K base), but a rebirth only adds +50% income and a new biome about x4-5. So every step takes longer than the one before, with one big wall (Cosmic, x39 speed). ([axeetech rebirth calculator](https://axeetech.com/steal-an-egg-rebirth-calculator/), unofficial.)

**How we measured it.** For every buy: its price in minutes of the kid's income at that moment (`pacing_sim.py`, 20 runs). After the coin pass the trend rose but had giveaways late: Thaw 10 at 5.6 h cost 7 minutes of income (like hour 1.5), gun 8 cost 10, gun 10 at 10.8 h cost 15, Rebirth 5 was easier than Rebirth 4. Every price after 40 minutes was then fitted toward a rising curve (0.3 x minutes^0.75: about 4 at 30 min, 11 at 2 h, 18 at 4 h, 27 at 7 h, 36 at 10 h), only where it was 20% easier or 30% harder than the curve, and every ladder made to at least double a step. A steeper curve (0.25 x minutes^0.82) and dearer trainers 6-7 pushed island 10 past 10 h with a third of kids unfinished at 14 h, so they were dropped: trainers gate islands, keep them near the curve.
